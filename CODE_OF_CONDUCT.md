# CivOS code of conduct

Participation must be open to people of different backgrounds, identities, abilities, ages, beliefs, and levels of experience. Everyone deserves respectful treatment while their claims and proposals remain open to substantive criticism.

## Expected conduct

Engage with the actual argument, identify evidence and assumptions, and give others room to clarify or correct their position. Describe disagreements accurately. Accept corrections without targeting the person who raised them. Ask before sharing information that could identify or expose somebody else.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate intimidation, and publication of another person's private information without consent are unacceptable. Repeated personal attacks and deliberate disruption are also unacceptable.

Respect for a participant does not require agreement with their claims. It is acceptable to question a method, reject an unsupported conclusion, or criticize an institution or practice. It is not acceptable to use that disagreement to demean, threaten, or harass a person or group.

## Scope and response

These standards apply in project-controlled spaces and when a person explicitly represents the project. Maintainers may moderate material or restrict participation in response to harmful conduct. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior, explain the relevant standard where practicable, and avoid unnecessary disclosure of personal details.

This repository does not currently list a verified confidential reporting address or a separate conduct panel. Do not post sensitive incident details in a public issue. For conduct in a GitHub issue or pull request, use GitHub's available reporting controls or contact a maintainer through a contact route they have explicitly published. A public issue may be used to request clarification of these standards without identifying affected people.

The project cannot promise confidentiality for public submissions, a fixed response time, or an independent appeal process it has not established. A participant may ask a maintainer to reconsider a moderation decision and explain the concern; this is a request for review, not a guarantee of a separate adjudicator.
